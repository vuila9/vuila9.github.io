Description

3D Tree is a community notice board shaped like a tree, embedded into this portfolio through an iframe. You don't need an account. Spin the tree, zoom in on any post, and react to it with a 👍 or 👎. Anyone can submit an image or a short text sign to the Memes or Wisdom board. Each submission is sent to my Discord for review, and once I approve it, it appears on the tree for everyone at once, with no refresh needed. Posts wrap around the trunk like real posters, and the tree grows taller as more posts are added. If yours wasn't approved, you'll find out why the next time you visit.

How it works

The app runs on Cloudflare at its own address, pole.3d-obj.workers.dev; this page only embeds it. The 3D scene is built with three.js: a single tree model, with each post laid onto the trunk's curved surface so it hugs the bark. Images are resized and compressed in your browser before they're sent, which also strips their metadata, such as camera details and location.

Everything else runs on Cloudflare. Images are stored in R2, and a single Durable Object holds the boards, the review queue and the reaction counts. It also keeps a live WebSocket connection open to every viewer, so new posts, likes and edits show up on every open page within a moment.

Reviews happen on Discord. Each submission arrives in a private channel with the image, its details, and any existing posts it looks like. I approve it onto a board, or reject it with a reason, using buttons on the message. Uploads are checked for duplicates using an image fingerprint that still matches mirrored and rotated copies.

To keep things fair, every submission must pass Cloudflare Turnstile. Each visitor can have only one post waiting for review at a time, and the queue has an overall cap. Visitors are told apart by a salted, hashed IP address; no address is stored as-is. Nicknames are checked against a reserved list and a banned list. The embed only loads on approved domains.

Features
  • No account needed: browse, react and submit straight from the page.
  • Real 3D posts: images and text signs wrap around the trunk, and the tree grows to make room for more.
  • Live updates: new posts and reactions appear for everyone instantly, with a notice and a button to jump to the new post.
  • Reviewed submissions: every post is approved or rejected on Discord, and visitors are told the outcome, with a reason if rejected.
  • Reactions: 👍 and 👎 on every post, or double-tap a post for a quick like, limited to once per post every 5 minutes.
  • Easy to explore: arrow keys, swipe to the next post, find a post by its ID, and a Cinematic mode that tours the tree on its own.
Future Improvements
  • More reactions: add a few emotes alongside 👍 and 👎.
  • Discord commands: manage posts (find, delete, add context) with slash commands straight from Discord.
  • Better iPhone support: a long press on a post doesn't open its menu yet on iOS Safari.
